* All application materials, including the application fee, must be received by the posted deadline. (Posted deadlines are for 11:59 PM U.S. Central Time Zone.) Late applications will not be accepted, and incomplete applications will be subject to denial.  International applicants are strongly encouraged to submit their applications early to allow sufficient time to seek their student visas.
Note : We strongly recommend that you and your recommenders not wait until the deadline to submit your application or letters of recommendation. Server traffic around the deadline may cause technical difficulties. It is highly recommended to submit all application materials one month before the deadline to expedite admission decisions.
*Applications to the Fifth Year Masters Programs are only open to current CS undergraduates enrolled in The Grainger College of Engineering who have junior standing (must have at least one year left of their undergraduate studies after admission into the program).
** Admissions decisions are released to applicants as the committee finalizes them. This is an ongoing process throughout the period between the application deadline and the decision deadline.
